CGCollegeGo

Privacy Policy

Applies to the CollegeGo app (Android and iOS) and the CollegeGo Faculty Desk website. Last updated: 21 October 2026.

CollegeGo is a campus app for students and staff of IIIT Surat. It is built and run by Vaibhav Raj (student developer). This page explains what information the app collects, why, who can see it, and how to delete it.

Who can use it

Only people with an IIIT Surat account (@iiitsurat.ac.in) can sign in, either with Google or with their institute email and a password. Email accounts must confirm the address from the institute inbox first.

What we collect

  • Account: your name, institute email and profile photo, from Google Sign-In or from the sign-up form. Passwords are handled by Firebase Authentication and are never stored by the app.
  • Student profile: enrollment number, branch, section, batch and specialization. Phone number and up to 3 emergency contacts, only if you add them.
  • Faculty profile: department, designation, office, office hours, phone and other details you choose to add.
  • Academic records provided by the institute: class roster, timetable, attendance, grades and exam schedules.
  • Things you submit: leave applications (including a medical report you upload), complaints and doubts, counselling requests, event excusal requests, lost & found posts and photos, reports you make about a post, and notices or announcements you post. If you block someone, that choice is kept only on your phone.
  • Location: only when you press SOS. While that alert is open your location is shared with the responders (campus doctor, wardens and committee members), so help can reach you. Location is never collected otherwise, and never in the background.
  • Notifications: a push token for your device, so the app can send class reminders, notices and SOS alerts.

We do not use advertising, ad tracking or analytics SDKs, and we do not sell or share your data for marketing.

How it is used

  • To show your timetable, attendance, grades, notices, exam schedule and other campus information.
  • To send requests (leave, complaints, counselling, excusals) to the faculty or staff who handle them.
  • To alert responders, and your emergency contacts, when you press SOS.
  • To send notifications you can switch off in Notification Settings.

Who can see it

  • You can see your own data.
  • Faculty and staff can see what they need for their role, for example a class's attendance, or a request addressed to them.
  • SOS alerts are visible to the responders listed above, and to your emergency contacts by SMS.
  • Lost & found posts and notices are visible to signed-in campus users. Reports about a post are visible only to faculty and admins, who review them.

Where it is stored

Data is stored in Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Storage and Cloud Functions). Push notifications go through Expo's push service and Apple / Google. SOS text messages may go through an SMS provider. These services process data only to provide these features. All data is sent over encrypted (HTTPS) connections.

Deleting your account

In the app, open Profile → Delete account. This permanently deletes:

  • your sign-in account and profile (phone, emergency contacts, push token)
  • your lost & found posts and photos
  • your complaints and counselling requests

Academic and safety records that belong to the institute (roster entry, attendance, grades, leave applications and SOS alert history) are kept as institute records.

You can also request deletion without the app: delete-account page.

Retention

Your data is kept while you have an account. Class announcements are hidden after 7 days. Institute records are kept as long as the institute requires.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes, and important changes are announced in the app.
