Delete your CollegeGo account

For the CollegeGo app (Android and iOS), by Vaibhav Raj.

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open CollegeGo and go to the Profile tab.
  2. Tap Delete account, then Delete forever.

Your account is deleted immediately.

Without the app

Email us from your institute email address. We delete the account within 7 days and reply to confirm.

Email a deletion request

What gets deleted

  • Your sign-in account and profile: name, photo, phone, emergency contacts and notification token.
  • Your lost & found posts and photos.
  • Your complaints and counselling requests.

What is kept

Records that belong to IIIT Surat stay with the institute: your roster entry, attendance, grades, leave applications and SOS alert history.
